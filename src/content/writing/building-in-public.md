For most of the last decade I built things quietly. A project would live on my laptop
for months, get a burst of weekend energy, and then quietly fade when the next idea
showed up. Nobody saw the work, so nobody missed it when it stopped.

Building in public is the opposite bet. Instead of waiting for a polished launch, I'm
posting the messy middle: the half-working prototype, the architecture I regretted, the
feature I cut. The work becomes a conversation instead of a reveal.

## The accountability is the point

The honest reason is accountability. When the log is public, an abandoned project is
visible. That small social pressure has done more for my follow-through than any
productivity system I've tried.

## What this site is

This is the log. The **Writing** section is where I think out loud, and the
**Projects** list is the running inventory of what I'm making. Some of it will work.
Some of it won't. All of it will be here.
