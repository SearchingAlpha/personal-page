I have rewritten this site three times in two years. A hand-rolled static generator, a
heavy framework I never needed, and now something deliberately boring. Each rewrite felt
urgent at the time. In hindsight, only one was worth it.

## The two rewrites I regret

The first two were procrastination in a nice outfit. Rebuilding the site felt like
progress, but it was really a way to avoid the harder work of actually writing and
shipping. New tooling is a very comfortable place to hide.

## The one that mattered

The rewrite that counted was the one where I stopped optimizing the build and started
optimizing the writing. I picked tools that got out of the way and made adding a post a
thirty-second job. Friction is the real enemy of a personal site — not the framework.

## The lesson

Your stack matters far less than whether publishing is easy. If adding a post is a
chore, you won't. Optimize for the thing you'll do most often.
